## Platform

web

## Users
Builders who like to see the parts: makers, ops engineers and technical founders who assemble automations node by node and tweak them.

## Product Purpose
NodeCraft: home page with a workflow preview, a node-based workflow builder (drag nodes, connect, AI chat helper), templates, a monitoring dashboard, team, pricing and settings, with an auth modal. React 18 + Vite; styles come from a theme object `C` (`src/theme.js`) plus a `GlobalStyle` component and inline styles. FastAPI backend.

## Positioning
Build automations from parts you can see and rearrange.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Colour lives in `C` and a few literals; re-mapping `C` re-themes most of the UI.
- Landing stats, testimonials and prices are marketing placeholders.

## Brand Commitments
- Name "NodeCraft".

## Product Principles
1. Parts are visible and nameable.
2. Assembly order is clear.
3. Hands-on, plain language.
